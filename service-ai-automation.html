Automation and AI for the Work That Repeats

Most businesses lose hours a week to work nobody chose to do — copying details between systems, compiling the same report, answering the same question. This is about removing those hours.

Plan Your Automation See What We Build

Automation and AI do different jobs

If a task follows a predictable rule — when an enquiry arrives, create a record and notify the right person — conventional automation handles it, reliably and cheaply. No AI needed.

AI earns its place with unstructured information: reading a document, sorting messy text, drafting a summary, answering from a body of material. It is very good at that and imperfect at all of it, which is why anything that commits your business to an action keeps a person in the loop. We will tell you which of the two your problem actually needs — often it is the simpler one.

Reduce the work your team repeats every day

Four ordinary examples of work that usually does not need a person doing it by hand.

Enquiry Routing

An enquiry arrives by form, email or WhatsApp. It is logged, categorised, assigned to the right person and acknowledged — without anyone watching an inbox.

Document Processing

Supplier invoices, delivery notes, filled-in forms. The details are extracted and put where they belong, with anything uncertain flagged for a person to check.

Recurring Reports

The Monday figures that someone currently assembles from three systems, compiled on schedule and sent to the people who need them.

Keeping Systems in Step

A customer's details change in one tool and follow into the others, so nobody types the same address into three places.
