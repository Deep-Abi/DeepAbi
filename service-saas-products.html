What makes something a SaaS product

SaaS means Software as a Service: customers use it online through an account instead of installing and running it themselves. The technical difference from a normal business application is who it serves. One company, or many separate ones — and if it is many, each customer's data must stay properly separated from the first line of code.

To be clear about what this service is: DeepAbi builds SaaS products for clients. We are not selling access to established SaaS products of our own.

Turn a product idea into an ongoing online service

The machinery a product needs before it can take its first paying customer.

Sign-Up Without You in the Room

A new customer creates an account, gets into the product and reaches something useful on their own. Onboarding is part of the product, not a support call.

A Workspace Per Customer

Each organisation gets its own space, its own members and its own data, kept separate by design rather than by a filter someone remembers to apply.

Plans, Payment and Changes

Tiers, sign-up, upgrades, downgrades and cancellations — including what a customer keeps access to when a payment fails.

Roles Inside Each Account

Owners, admins and ordinary members, so your customers can invite their own team without you administering it for them.

A Back Office for You

Your own view of accounts, plans and usage — so support can answer a question and you can see how the product is being used.
